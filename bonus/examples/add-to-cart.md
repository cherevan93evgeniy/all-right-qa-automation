# BONUS-01 — Add a product to the cart

Preconditions:
- The published SauceDemo standard user is available and starts with an empty cart.

Steps:
1. Sign in as the standard user.
2. Add Sauce Labs Backpack.
3. Open the cart.

Expected result:
- The cart page shows exactly one item.
- The visible item is Sauce Labs Backpack with a unit price of $29.99 and a quantity of one.
